## Deadline

Official rules §5: the entry period ends **11:59 PM Pacific Standard Time, Friday 2 October 2026**.

| Reading | UK time (BST) |
|---|---|
| PST as written (UTC−8) | Sat 3 Oct, 08:59 |
| PDT, what California is actually on (UTC−7) | Sat 3 Oct, 07:59 |

**Our target: submitted by Friday 2 Oct, evening UK time.** NVIDIA's emails just say "October 2"
and "late submissions will not be accepted", and the rules disclaim responsibility for late or
lost entries. Don't plan to use the overnight margin.

## Submit

Form: https://airtable.com/appbWMw3ySORLZTgV/pagLOxwVLzgiOaunm/form

- **Demo video or link to site**: show it actually working, 60–90 s is ideal.
- **Short description**: what it does and why we built it.

## Judging

1. **Agent successfully deployed**: runs end to end without crashing; real engineering
   (multi-step reasoning, tool use, memory, long-running) rather than a one-shot prompt.
2. **Innovation & creativity**: a novel combination of tools, models or data; ambition.
3. **Real-world value**: solves a real problem; who it helps and how, in one sentence.

## Eligibility

UK resident, 18+, individuals only (no corporate entries). NVIDIA employees, contractors and
their households are excluded.

## After

- Winners announced on or around **6 Oct 2026**, notified within 7 days; the winner has
  7 days to reply and return the release form.
- Winners demo at the NVIDIA Build-a-Claw booth in London on **14 Oct 2026**.
- 1st: GTC Berlin pass + DGX Spark Founders Edition + booth demo. 2nd: GTC Berlin pass + booth demo.
