# Pinch, variant B: Inspector with lens

**Concept.** A teardrop-bodied green lobster with heavy upper lids. He holds a magnifying lens in his smaller right claw (real lobsters have one big crusher claw and one small cutter claw). The lens is the camera light: clear when Alibi isn't looking, green while it is.

**Why it charms.** The lids do the acting. Half-lowered they look dry; tilted outward, kind; closed with a lash line, content. In side-eye he raises the lens to one eye, and the glass shows that eye magnified.

**Why it reads small.** At 16–23 px there are five solid shapes: body, a V pincer, the lens ring with its stem, and two flat-topped eyes. The flat tops keep the lids.

**How it animates.** There are 24 `<g id>` parts, each with a pivot in `pivots.json`. Every pose is transforms on those parts, plus a mouth swap.
